Tio1608-D gain and phantom
C3 Life or R Remote
The Wing can't change the Tio's gain or phantom power, but C3 Life can: on Mixer, tap a channel fed by the Tio, then Input. It uses the same Yamaha commands as R Remote: gain (−6 to +66 dB), +48 V phantom and the low cut on each input. Set the Tio's address on Mixer's Tio1608-D card; it starts as Watch only. Yamaha's free R Remote app still works alongside it, on the C3 Life server.
One-time setup at the booth
  1. On the C3 Life server, install Dante Controller, Dante Virtual Soundcard and R Remote V6. R Remote needs Dante Controller.
  2. On the Tio's back panel (with the power off): turn Quick Config off. Quick Config is for Yamaha TF desks, and it would wipe the Dante routing every time the Tio is switched on.
  3. Set DIP switches 7 and 8 to Resume, not Refresh, so the Tio keeps its gain and phantom settings after a power cut. Refresh waits for a Yamaha desk to send them.
  4. Set DIP switches 5 and 6 to Static IP manual, then give it 192.168.10.41 in R Remote.
  5. Plug the switch into the Tio's PRIMARY port. Gain control only works through PRIMARY.
  6. Give it a unique UNIT ID (1 is fine, as there's only one Tio).
R Remote V6 supports Windows 10 and 11, including Windows 10 IoT Enterprise LTSC on the C3 Life server. V7 adds newer macOS, but Yamaha doesn't list the Tio for it, so test before relying on it.
