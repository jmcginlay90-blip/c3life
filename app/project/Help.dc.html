The big picture
A small computer in the booth (the C3 Life server, a Dell OptiPlex Micro running Windows) talks to the Wing, Dante, ProPresenter, Playback and the lights. R Remote and Dante Controller run on it too. Every phone, iPad and laptop on the booth Wi-Fi opens c3life.local and sees the same thing, live. Nothing needs the internet. When there is internet, it syncs Planning Center and emails reports.
What the colours mean
Blue: connected and working
Orange: something is disconnected or needs attention
Grey: left out on purpose (e.g. the events Mac)
Amber: live, selected or locked
Red screen: the room is over the level limit
Green screen: the MD is talking
