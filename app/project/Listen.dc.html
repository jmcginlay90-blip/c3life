Using hearing aids?
If your hearing aids connect to your phone by Bluetooth (most made in the last few years do: iPhone "Made for iPhone", Android "ASHA" or "LE Audio"), Listen plays straight into them, like a phone call.
  1. Check your hearing aids are connected to your phone (iPhone: Settings › Accessibility › Hearing Devices).
  2. Open Listen and tap Play. Use the phone's volume buttons.
  3. In the room with the speaker: the sound in your aids is 1 to 3 seconds behind, so you hear an echo. Set your aids to stream only (often called "mute microphones" in the hearing aid app), or use the hall's hearing loop (T setting) instead.
  4. In another room or at home: it just works.
Turn on Subtitles while playing the speech source if you'd like to read along too.
