People listen from home through a free private network app, Tailscale, not the open internet. Only people you invite can join it. From home they can open Listen and hear what their group allows for home, and nothing else: no controls, no mixes, no other screens.
Turn it on (once, on the C3 Life server)
  1. Install Tailscale on the C3 Life server and sign in with the church's account.
  2. On the C3 Life server, run Install-C3Life.ps1 -ListenFromHome. It lets Tailscale devices reach Listen, and nothing else. (Ask whoever set up the C3 Life server to do this step.)
  3. On Listen settings › Groups, tick Can listen from home for each group that may.
  4. For each listener: invite them to the church's Tailscale, then have them install Tailscale on their phone and open http://c3life.local.
To stop someone, remove them from the church's Tailscale, or take them out of the group. To stop all home listening, untick it on every group.
