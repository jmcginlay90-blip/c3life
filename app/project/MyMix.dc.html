You can only change your own mix ({{busName}}). Nothing here reaches the main speakers, the stream or anyone else's mix. A tap that jumps more than 6 dB only moves 6 dB, so nothing hurts your ears. Double-tap a pan to centre it.
MIX: one strip per channel, with names, colours and icons from the Wing. Drag a fader, tap ON to take a channel out of your mix, and drag the pan. Your own volume and MUTE are on the right.

MCA: four group faders. Tap GROUP on a channel to put it in MCA 1 to 4, then move the whole group at once. MCA SPILL shows just that group.

USER: choose which channels you see (the eye), put them in your order, and add blank spaces. RESET puts the layout back.
